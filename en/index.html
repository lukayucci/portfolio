03 — case studies

Three systems, three different problems. Same standard: make them work.

01

Kael CRM

Multi-tenant CRM for real estate teams

In production

Listings, leads and deals in one system. Multi-tenant from the data model up, with cookie-based auth, AI-generated content (local or cloud) and PDF reporting.

Context

Small agencies run on spreadsheets, WhatsApp and duplicated listings. The goal was to unify lead capture, follow-up and sales material without mixing data across organizations.

What I built

  • 01 Real multi-tenancy: every query is scoped to an organization, backed by isolation tests.
  • 02 JWT auth in httpOnly cookies, owner/agent roles and rate limiting.
  • 03 Kanban pipeline, contacts, comparables and listings in a single flow.
  • 04 AI-assisted copy and reporting; supports local Ollama and LM Studio so agency data never leaves its network. PDF export.
  • 05 Tokko integration and comparable extraction from listing portals.

Outcome It centralizes an agency's daily operation: listings, leads and follow-up in a single place.

02

ZERO

Shadow IT & Data Leak Finder

Public repo

Defensive audit SaaS that finds Shadow IT and data leaks, ranks findings by severity and ships client-ready executive reports.

Context

Companies do not know which services, buckets or credentials they expose by accident. The tool automates discovery and turns it into an actionable report.

What I built

  • 01 Defensive OSINT collection: subdomains, buckets, logs and exposed configuration.
  • 02 AI analysis and risk scoring across five severity levels.
  • 03 Custom auth with sessions, plans and per-scan credits.
  • 04 Dashboard with scan history and downloadable Markdown/JSON reports.
  • 05 SSRF guards and domain validation before every run.

Outcome Turns a manual review that took hours into a repeatable flow, with reports that can be delivered without hand-editing.
